Minutes from Meeting 1
During the first 10 minutes of the Scrum meeting, the team described their progress for Sprint 1, which included what they finished, issues they had with executing all of their work, and what blocked them. For 15 minutes, we went through some of the more important features that they finished (i.e. cashier and manager screens, database connection, and most important functions of the web application). The team shared those features with some short explanations or demonstrations where needed. The next 10 minutes was about the communication gaps which pointed to the need for more regular communications and better estimates related to tasks, identifying what to focus on later. Team members provided their feedback related to what should be adjusted and what continued to work. The group was then able to shift into more effective workflows, frequent updates, and refining estimates in the backlog to increase efficiencies for the last 10 minutes of the meeting. In the last 5 minutes of the meeting, the team was able to identify the most important takeaways, develop clear Sprint 2 action items, and make sure the team was clear on what was next.
